--- Mon 14:12, Claude → Maya (private)
Here are three options. The distances between them are my own rough estimates, not checked against a map.

1. **Borough Market (SE1, by London Bridge station)**
   - Lunch: the market has lots of food stalls and nearby restaurants. The official [visit page](https://boroughmarket.org.uk/visit-us/) gives Saturday hours as 9am–5pm. It says hours can change over the festive period.
   - Walk: the Thames Path along the South Bank. [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/a-perfect-day-in-london) says it passes Shakespeare's Globe, Tate Modern and the Southbank Centre, up to the London Eye.

2. **Covent Garden (WC2)**
   - Lunch: the Piazza and the streets around it have many restaurants.
   - Walk: down to the Embankment, then over Waterloo Bridge to the South Bank. A [Ramblers route](https://www.ramblers.org.uk/go-walking/routes/walk-round-londons-west-end-and-south-bank) covers this, but I couldn't open the page, so I only have its search-result description.

3. **Camden Market (NW1)**
   - Lunch: the market's food stalls. It's open seven days a week and is busy.
   - Walk: along Regent's Canal to Primrose Hill, then through Regent's Park. [Walking Britain's route](https://www.walkingbritain.co.uk/walk-3129-description) from Camden Town to Baker Street is 2.5 miles (4.1 km) and takes about 1 hour 20 minutes. The Primrose Hill summit has views of the London Eye, the Shard and St Paul's.

**Rough distances between them (straight-line)**
- Borough Market to Covent Garden: about 2.5 km (1.5 miles)
- Covent Garden to Camden: about 3.5 km (2 miles)
- Borough Market to Camden: about 6 km (3.7 miles)

Borough and Covent Garden are close enough to walk between. Camden is a short Tube ride from either.

**Timing:** sunset on 28 Nov is around 4pm. That's from memory, and I didn't check it. A walk starting at 2pm will finish around dusk. The riverside walks are lit in the evening, but Regent's Park closes around sunset (per [Komoot](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/5773139) and similar sources). That makes

Camden the weakest fit for a 2pm walk. The Primrose Hill views and the park would be in fading light. Borough Market or Covent Garden suits better, because the riverside route stays open and lit after dark.

The Komoot detail about the park closing came from a search summary. I didn't open the page, so check the park's closing time before you go.

If you want one pick, I'd go for Borough Market, then the South Bank walk. The market is open until 5pm, and the walk starts right outside it.
