Two Kirkstall pubs fit, and one pub nearby is a weaker fit. I could only check the listed hours, not that the pubs trade as normal this November. Ring ahead before you go.

**Kirkstall Bridge Inn** (12 Bridge Road, LS5 3BW)
- It's open Sunday 12:00–23:30, according to [Kirkstall Brewery's own page](https://www.kirkstallbrewery.com/pubs/kirkstall-bridge-inn).
- It has a waterside beer garden on the edge of the River Aire. Other sources describe three outdoor areas: the garden, a tent and a marquee ([search summary of Discover Leeds and others](https://discoverleeds.co.uk/things-to-see-and-do/food-and-drink/kirkstall-bridge-inn/)). I didn't open that Discover Leeds page.
- The brewery warns the garden "gets a bit wet sometimes" and links to a flooding story, so November could be soggy. The tent and marquee may help.
- [CAMRA](https://www1.camra.org.uk/pubs/178007) lists Sunday food noon–5pm, as seen in search results only.
- Phone: 0113 278 4044.

**Vesper Gate** (Abbey Road, LS5 3NG)
- [CAMRA](https://camra.org.uk/pubs/vesper-gate-leeds-178029) lists it as open Sunday noon–11pm, with food noon–5pm.
- It has a grassed beer garden at the rear.
- CAMRA says it's near Kirkstall Abbey but doesn't mention the river or canal. I didn't confirm the distance, so treat it as "near the Abbey" and not "by the river".

**Abbey Inn** (99 Pollard Lane, Newlay, LS13 1EQ) is a weaker fit.
- [CAMRA](https://camra.org.uk/pubs/abbey-inn-leeds-177301) lists Sunday noon–11pm, with food noon–5pm.
- It has "ample seating outside".
- It's in Newlay, not Kirkstall, and CAMRA doesn't say it's near the Abbey. I included it only because of its name.

I haven't checked whether any of these has special November hours or closes its garden in winter.
