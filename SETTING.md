# Setting: a modern city neighbourhood (present day) — supersedes the pre-industrial flavour in SPEC.md
Working name: **Larkfield** (a mixed inner-city neighbourhood of a larger, unnamed city). Systems, contract and
quality bar stay; content, rates and texture change. Realism benchmark: a present-day Western/UK/US urban
neighbourhood. Everything should feel like real contemporary life — mundane, specific, sometimes funny.

## Place
- Map ~ 12-16 city blocks with streets (cars, bus stops, one metro/tram station at the edge = gateway to the
  wider city), a small park, a canal or railway line. Mixed density: **apartment blocks** (many units each;
  a unit = household), terraced/row houses, a few detached houses, ground-floor shops under flats.
- Places: cafés, corner shop, supermarket, pub/bar(s), restaurant(s), takeaway, gym, GP clinic + pharmacy,
  primary school, secondary school (or bus to one), nursery, office building(s), coworking space, a small
  hospital or A&E off-map (via station), police station (or patrols), community centre, library, church/mosque/
  temple (multi-faith), barber/salon, laundrette, construction site, warehouse/delivery hub, nightclub,
  park, playground, bus stops. Buildings change hands: shops close and reopen as new businesses (gentrification).
- **Off-map world**: many people work, study, shop, date, visit family "in the city" (they leave via the station
  and come back). Commuters/visitors from outside also pass through. People move in/out frequently.

## People (~500 initial, can range 350-900)
- Diverse ages, ethnic backgrounds and name origins (realistic mix for a modern city), household types:
  families, couples, flatshares of young professionals/students, single parents, retirees living alone,
  multigenerational families. Tenure: renting (most), mortgage, owned outright, social housing.
- Jobs (wages & hours realistic, monthly-ish rent): office/remote workers (tech, admin, finance, marketing),
  retail, hospitality (barista, bartender, chef, waiter), gig delivery riders/drivers, nurses/doctors (often
  off-map hospital shifts incl. nights), teachers, care workers, tradespeople, builders, cleaners, police,
  shop owners, students (school/university), artists/musicians, unemployed, retired, stay-at-home parents.
  Career progression, promotions, layoffs, side hustles, starting a business, working from home.
- Money: monthly rent/mortgage, bills, groceries, eating out, savings, debt/credit cards, benefits/pensions.
  Cost-of-living pressure drives stress, moving out, flatsharing, second jobs.

## Social texture
- Phones: people text/message friends not physically present (a cheap "remote contact" channel that keeps
  relationships alive at lower strength than meeting), group chats, social media posts (public feed: brags,
  rants, photos at the pub) that spread gossip faster and wider, and occasional viral moments.
- Dating apps produce dates with strangers (incl. off-map people who may move in), situationships, ghosting,
  moving in together before/without marriage, cohabitation common, marriage later (median ~30-33),
  divorce/separation realistic (~35-45% of marriages eventually), blended families, LGBTQ+ couples normal.
- Neighbours often barely know each other in big blocks (low familiarity) — community forms around
  places: the café regulars, the gym crowd, the pub quiz team, school-gate parents, the dog walkers.
- Hobbies/clubs: five-a-side football, running club, book club, pub quiz, choir, gaming, climbing, church groups.
- Events: street party/festival, neighbourhood fair, New Year, school holidays, football match nights,
  council planning protests, a new development, power cut, heatwave, flood, burst pipe, local election.

## Calibration targets (present-day urban)
- Life expectancy at birth ~80 (deaths mostly 70+: heart disease, cancer, dementia, stroke; few accidents,
  overdoses, rare violent deaths). Infant mortality ~0.3-0.5%.
- Fertility ~1.4-1.8 children per woman; mean age at first birth ~30-31; many childless.
- Population churn: ~10-20% of residents move in/out per year; young adults most mobile; families move to
  suburbs when kids arrive; population stable-ish driven by migration more than births.
- Unemployment ~4-8%; many commute out; gini of wealth ~0.5-0.7, income ~0.3-0.4.
- Crime: mostly theft, bike theft, shoplifting, burglary, vandalism, antisocial behaviour, drug dealing,
  fraud/scams, occasional assault after a night out; homicide extremely rare.
- Time: 1 game-year still = 112 days (each day represents ~3.26 real days of life); age 1:1 with game years.
  Weekdays/weekend (5+2), nine-to-five plus shift work, night life on Fri/Sat.
