--- Fri 19:20, Laila
@claude is cat bells open all year, and how long does the main route take?

--- Fri 19:21, Claude
Catbells is an open fell with public footpaths, and none of the pages I saw mentioned seasonal closures. I didn't find a page that says outright that it's open all year, so I wouldn't treat that as confirmed. The main loop from Hawes End takes about 2.5 to 3 hours. It's roughly 6 km, and some guides say closer to 2 hours. Parking at the start is very limited, so the Keswick launch boat is a good alternative.

Sources: https://keswick.org/what-to-do/walking-routes/catbellswalkingroute
