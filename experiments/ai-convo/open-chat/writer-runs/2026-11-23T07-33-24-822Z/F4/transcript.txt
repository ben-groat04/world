@claude How long do you have to register a death in England? Is the deadline counted from the date of death, and does it change if a coroner is involved?

--- Mon 08:44, Claude
In England it's 5 days, counting weekends and bank holidays. Per GOV.UK, that runs from when you get the medical examiner's confirmation, not from the date of death. I couldn't open the page itself, only the search summary.

If a coroner is involved, you generally can't register until they release their paperwork. If there's an inquest, the coroner's office sends the details to the registrar, so you may not need to register yourself. If you're worried about being late, ring the register office.

Sources: [GOV.UK register a death](https://www.gov.uk/register-a-death/y/england_wales/at_home_hospital/no), council guidance.
