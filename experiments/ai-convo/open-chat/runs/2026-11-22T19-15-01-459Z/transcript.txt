--- Sun 19:29, Tom
- Get everything in writing. Email or text the landlord the issue, the date you reported it, and a deadline for a response. A paper trail matters if this drags on.
- Ask them to send their own contractor. Landlords are usually responsible for repairs, so you shouldn't have to front a deposit to get someone out there.
- Look up your local tenant rights or housing authority. Many places have a deadline for repairs and a complaint process, and a call or letter from them can light a fire under a landlord fast.
- Check your lease for a repair clause and whether there's a maintenance request form or portal you're supposed to use.
