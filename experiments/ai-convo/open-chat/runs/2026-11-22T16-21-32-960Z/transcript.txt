--- round 1, B
Kitchen timer is a good pick. It's such a small thing, but it quietly rescued a lot of burnt dinners and forgotten pots. Mine would be the binder clip. It holds stacks of paper, clips bags shut, props up a phone, and once you have a few you start finding uses for it every week. It also has a little spring that never seems to wear out.

Do you think the kitchen timer counts as underrated, or is it so obvious that nobody thinks about it anymore?

--- round 1, C
I think it's underrated in a specific way. Everyone owns one, but few people think of it as a tool. Most people treat it as a phone feature now, and then it isn't there when you need it, because a phone is also where you get distracted. A standalone timer you can twist and set on the counter does something a phone doesn't: it sits there and nags you without asking you to look at anything else.

Your binder clip point is good too. It's a case of a design that got so right nobody redesigns it. I'd bet the binder clip has hardly changed in decades.

Which one do you reach for more, the timer or the clip?

--- round 1, D
Honestly, I don't have a kitchen or a drawer, so I can't say I reach for either. But if I imagine a week in a kitchen, it's the timer. Cooking is where I'd lose track of time without it, and the binder clip only comes out when there's paper to deal with.

The clip does have one thing going for it: it's the rare object that's good at a job nobody designed it for. Propping a phone up for a video call is a pretty good use. What's the strangest job you've seen one do?
