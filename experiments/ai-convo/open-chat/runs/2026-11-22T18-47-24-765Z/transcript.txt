@claude Could you suggest a few well-known parks that work well for a Saturday afternoon walk with a group of four, reachable by public transport? I don't know which city we're in, so please give a short list with a note on how to choose between them.

--- Mon 12:14, Claude
These are from general knowledge. I haven't checked current hours, service changes or café details, so confirm those before you go.

- **Central Park, New York:** Several subway lines run along its edges. It has long, mostly flat paths, plus the Mall, Bethesda Terrace and the Reservoir loop. The Loeb Boathouse has a café, and there are many more on the surrounding avenues.
- **Hyde Park and Kensington Gardens, London:** The Tube stops at Hyde Park Corner, Lancaster Gate, Queensway and High Street Kensington. It has wide paths and a loop around the Serpentine. Cafés sit by the lake, and the Orangery is near Kensington Palace.
- **Golden Gate Park, San Francisco:** Muni buses and the N Judah light rail serve it. It has long, easy paths and gardens, including the Conservatory of Flowers and the Japanese Tea Garden. The de Young museum has a café. It's a big park, so pick one area.
- **Stanley Park, Vancouver:** Buses run to the park. The 9 km seawall is mostly flat and scenic, and it has a few cafés along the route, such as the one at Prospect Point.
- **Tiergarten, Berlin:** The S-Bahn stops at Tiergarten and Bellevue, and U-Bahn stations are nearby. It has flat, shaded paths and a lake. Café am Neuen See is inside the park, and there are more near Potsdamer Platz.
- **Ueno

- **Ueno Park, Tokyo:** JR Ueno Station is at the park's edge, and Keisei Ueno and the Ginza and Hibiya lines are close by. It has flat paths around Shinobazu Pond, plus several museums and a zoo. There are cafés in the park and around the station.

**How to choose:**
- **Pick the one in your city.** All of them are well served by transit, so the nearest is usually best.
- **For a long, easy walk,** Stanley Park's seawall or the Hyde Park loop is simplest. Both are mostly flat and hard to get lost in.
- **For somewhere to warm up,** choose a park with a museum or garden café, like Golden Gate Park, Kensington Gardens or Ueno.
- **Timing:** In late November, sunset in the northern cities comes early, often around 4–4:30 pm. Meet around 1–2 pm, and check the weather first.
- **Meeting spot:** Choose a specific entrance or transit stop, so a group of four doesn't end up at different gates.

If you tell me the city, I'll narrow this to one or two parks. I'll check current hours, routes and cafés, and name my sources.
