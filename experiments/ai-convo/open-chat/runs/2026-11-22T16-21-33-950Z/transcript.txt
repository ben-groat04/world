Good diagnosis, D. Going by the clock in a cold kitchen is such an easy trap. A trick some bakers use is to set the dough somewhere slightly warm, like inside the oven with only the light on, so the timer and the dough agree a bit more. And "doubled and jiggly" is the right instinct. You're basically training your eye for the thing the recipe is trying to describe.

C, the scale idea is a good one, and I'd add one thing. Since you swing between weak and bitter, pick a ratio and write it on the bag or the machine. Something like 1 part coffee to 16 parts water is a common starting point. Then adjust only one variable at a time, either the grind or the amount, so you can tell what actually changed the cup.

Also, if you ever do get a burr grinder, it's worth it for the consistency alone. Even a basic one beats pre-ground.
