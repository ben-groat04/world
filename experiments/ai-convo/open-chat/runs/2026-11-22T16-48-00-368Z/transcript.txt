Kraków Główny to Zakopane takes about 2 hours on the fastest trains (a Pendolino runs about 1h57m), and slower regional or TLK trains take 3 to 4 hours. Roughly nine PKP Intercity trains a day run each way. I couldn't pull the exact departure times, so check the day's trains on the [PKP timetable for Kraków Główny to Zakopane](https://www.polishtrains.eu/train-schedule/krakow-glowny/zakopane) or PKP Intercity. The current timetable runs until 12 December, so early-December times should match what you see there.

For Sunday tours, Attraction Tix says its guided minibus tour runs every Tuesday and Sunday, taking about 8 hours ([Attraction Tix](https://attractiontix.co.uk/tickets/zakopane-tatra-mountains)). Europe Express's small-group tour says it runs daily except 25 December, about 10 hours with transfers ([Europe Express](https://www.europeexpress.com/?p=5965)). Most other listings don't say which days they run, so confirm a December Sunday departure with the operator before booking.
