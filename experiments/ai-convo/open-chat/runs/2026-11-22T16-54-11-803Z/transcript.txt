Typical hot-tub cabin rates in Leavenworth run about $300 to $700 a night in winter, based on listing snapshots from Expedia and Hotels.com. Expedia's Leavenworth page shows a range of roughly $231 to $698, and a riverfront hot-tub cabin was $295 a night for a late-December stay. Ouray is similar or a bit cheaper: one winter Expedia listing was $375 a night, and Savvy's Ouray house listings run about $275 to $775. These are past snapshots, not 2027 prices, and I couldn't confirm any current winter deals. Since 2027 dates won't be bookable until later, check Airbnb, VRBO, or Expedia closer to the time. Splitting a $300 cabin four ways for two nights is about $150 each, which fits your $250 budget, but a $450+ cabin probably won't.
