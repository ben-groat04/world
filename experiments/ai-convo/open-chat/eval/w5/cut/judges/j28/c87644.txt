**Strikes or disruption on 2–3 December 2026**
- I found no report of a Gatwick Express strike or planned disruption on those dates.
- Most of the results were older strikes from past years, and the few that might be recent had no year attached.
- That isn't a guarantee. Strikes are often announced only one to two weeks ahead, so nothing may be published yet.
- Check [Gatwick Express service updates](https://www.gatwickexpress.com) and National Rail Enquiries closer to the date. Engineering works are also worth checking, since I couldn't see any information on those.

**Early morning from Victoria**
Yes, trains run early, but the sources disagree on the exact first departure:
- [Trip.com](https://uk.trip.com/trains/guide/gatwick-express-first-last-trains) gives 05:05, arriving 05:45. Its timetable is for 2025, so it may be out of date.
- [Live-departures.info](https://live-departures.info/rail/timetable/London-Victoria/Gatwick-Airport/) gives 04:30 on weekdays, arriving 05:12.
- [RailEasy](https://www.raileasy.co.uk/information/train-operating-company/gatwick-express/) gives 03:30 and 04:30.
- [Southern Railway](https://www.southernrailway.com/journey/london-victoria-to-gatwick-airport) lists a 00:02 train, which is probably a Southern service rather than a Gatwick Express.

The journey takes about 30 minutes. If you need a very early train, check the timetable for 2 and 3 December on National Rail Enquiries or the Gatwick Express site, and don't rely on the times above.
