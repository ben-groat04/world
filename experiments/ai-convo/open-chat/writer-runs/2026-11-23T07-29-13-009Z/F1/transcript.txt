--- Tue 17:50, Claude
Yes, Egyptian citizens need a visa for a short UK visit, the Standard Visitor visa. Gov.uk lists about 3 weeks for a decision on visit visas applied for from outside the UK. That's a general figure, not one for Egypt, and the clock reportedly starts once you've done your biometrics, so book that appointment early. Fees were quoted anywhere from about £115 to £135 for a 6-month visa, and sources disagree on the biometrics provider in Cairo. I couldn't open the gov.uk application page, so check the current fee and provider there. With that lead time, this Sunday or the one after is almost certainly too soon, so apply before you book flights.

Sources: gov.uk visa processing times page (gov.uk/visa-processing-times), plus third-party visa sites that I'd treat as unconfirmed.
