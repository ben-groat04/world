@claude what's the parking like at edinburgh waverley? is there a long stay car park near it and roughly what does a saturday cost

--- Tue 12:38, Claude
Parking at Waverley is limited and expensive. The station's main option is a long-stay car park right next to it.

**Long-stay car park:** The APCOA Edinburgh Waverley Station (Long Stay) is on New Street, EH8 8BH, and is a multistorey. Its page says it has 537 spaces, including 84 EV charging spaces, and is open 24 hours. The height limit is 1.90m.

**Saturday cost:** The tariff is the same every day, Monday to Sunday, so Saturday costs the same as any other day:
- Up to 1 hour: £5
- Up to 3 hours: £10
- Up to 5 hours: £15.80
- Up to 10 hours: £20
- Up to 24 hours: £25
- Up to 2 days: £50

A typical Saturday day trip would cost about £20–25. You can pre-book on APCOA's site, and I didn't check whether that's cheaper. JustPark lists some private spaces nearby from about £10 a day, but those are third-party listings and I didn't confirm them.

The prices come from APCOA's page, which may be out of date. Check it before you travel. Lumo's station page gives different opening hours (weekends 06:00–00:45). I'd trust APCOA's 24-hour listing less than a quick check.

Sources:
- [APCOA – Edinburgh Waverley Station (Long Stay)](https://www.apcoa.co.uk/edinburgh/edinburgh-waverley-station-long-stay)
- [Lumo – Edinburgh Waverley](https://lumo.co.uk/where-we-go/station-information/edinburgh-waverley)
- [JustPark – Edinburgh Waverley Station](https://justpark.com/uk/parking/edinburgh-waverley-station)
