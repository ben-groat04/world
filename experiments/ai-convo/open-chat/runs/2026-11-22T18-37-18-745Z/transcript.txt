--- Sun 19:26, Tom
Red lentil soup
- 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 garlic cloves, all chopped small
- Soften in a splash of oil for about 5 mins
- Add 1 cup red lentils (rinsed), 1 tsp cumin, a pinch of chilli flakes, and 1.2L stock
- Simmer for about 20 mins until the lentils break down
- Blend if you want it smooth (I don't bother, I just mash it a bit), then finish with lemon juice and salt
