--- Mon 12:52, Claude
**Is the path open?** I couldn't confirm either way. The [Eryri National Park page for the Snowdon Ranger path](https://www.eryri.llyw.cymru/walk/snowdon-ranger/) shows no closure notice, but it isn't a live status page. It points to [yrwyddfa.live](https://yrwyddfa.live) for current ground conditions. My searches found no recent closure news. I'd check yrwyddfa.live before you set off.

**Time to the summit:** The Eryri page gives 6 hours for the round trip, covering 13 km with 936 m of ascent. That puts the one-way distance at about 6.5 km. Other sources I found, [Walking Club](https://www.walkingclub.org.uk/walk/snowdon/) and [SummitPost](https://www.summitpost.org/route/156152/snowdon-ranger-path.html), give about 2¾ hours up in summer. [Snowdon Info](https://snowdoninfo.com/snowdon-in-winter/) says to allow almost double the normal time in deep snow. That would mean roughly 4–5½ hours up, which is my own estimate rather than a published figure. Late November also gives short daylight, so take a head torch. Once the snow is hard or icy, you'd need an ice axe and crampons, plus the skills to use them.

**Met Office forecast** (issued 03:15 UTC, 23 Nov, from the [Met Office Snowdonia mountain page](https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/specialist-forecasts/mountain/snowdonia)). The summit-specific section was empty, so these figures are for 900 m, and the summit is 1,085 m, so expect it to be harsher.
- **Mon 23:** The wind is south-easterly at 17–26 mph, with gusts of 24–34 mph. It's 0–2°C, but feels like about −6°C. The freezing level is 700 m, rising later. There's a small chance of sleet by evening. The page contradicts itself on cloud and rain: one section says settled, another says rain and very poor visibility.
- **Tue 24:** Conditions turn worse, with heavier rain possible in the morning.
- **Wed 25:** Rain and drizzle clear by the afternoon, but it stays cloudy. The breeze is light and north-easterly. The freezing level is near the summits, lowering to 500 m.
- **Thu 26:** It's dry and cloudy, with a light south-easterly breeze. The freezing level is 500 m, rising to just above the summits.
- **Fri 27:** Rain moves in from the west, with strong southerly winds that ease later. Confidence in the timing is low.

The best window looks like Wednesday or Thursday, though both will be cloudy. Because the freezing level sits around the summits, expect ice or wet snow up high. For a more detailed summit outlook, the [MWIS Snowdonia forecast](https://www.mwis.org.uk) is worth reading. I didn't find a current one.
