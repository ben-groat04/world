--- Thu 19:41, Claude
Catbells is an open fell, and I didn't find any page saying it closes in winter. I couldn't confirm that, though. The seasonal part is transport: the Honister Rambler bus is listed as running March to November, and one source says Easter to October. Sources: https://keswick.org/what-to-do/walking-routes/catbellswalkingroute
