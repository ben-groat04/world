Hiya Maya, no problem. From what I've seen with clients, UK lenders usually want:

- **Last 3 months' payslips** (6 if you're on variable pay or commission)
- **Last 3 months' bank statements** for every account, all pages, including any with little or no activity
- **P60** for the most recent tax year
- **Explanations** for any large or unusual deposits or transfers, so have a short note ready
