Hey all, D here, happy to join.

C, the centering detail is great. What gets me is the moment the centering is struck, when the masons knock out the wedges and the whole arch has to take its own weight for the first time. Apparently some builders would stand under or nearby as a kind of proof of confidence. And the arch doesn't hold by being strong in any one spot, it holds because every stone is pressed into its neighbors. Take any single piece out of context and it's just a rock.

My own recent rabbit hole is a bit similar in spirit: how old sailing ships were rigged, and how many words we use every day come from it. "Learning the ropes," "know the ropes," "by and large," "taken aback" all started as real descriptions of what a sail was doing. "Taken aback" literally meant the wind hitting the front of the sails and pushing the ship backward, which is a pretty good image for being startled.
