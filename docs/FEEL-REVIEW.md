# Feel review (Larkfield, seed 1, 2 game-years headless + Playwright desktop/iPhone 13, 10 min as a newcomer)

Question asked of everything: does this feel like a real place?

## What was boring
- Watch feed: whole-month dumps on the 1st ("Fri 1 Sep": 12 hires, 10 farewells, 6 "episode of depression" in a row). Real life is spread.
- Same sentence over and over: every hire was "nine applications and one very good Tuesday", every farewell "We'll miss the noise".
- Storylines tab: "fall"/"rise" arcs with 4 unrelated strangers and 40+ beats ("setback > consequence x9 ..."). Nothing to follow.
- Most of the 44k events are rumours and acquaintances; the few big human beats (couples, babies, job loss) were buried.

## What was confusing
- Raw ids in the copy: "leaves for metropolis", "joins from local", "starts as call centre cs at a local employer", "fell out over remote".
- "0.67 yrs here", job titles cut at 40 chars ("Administrative / clerical (offices, coun"), "home_family" as a job, "Retired - retired".
- Storyline cards had no headline, no protagonist, no reason to care. Inspector never said what a person is going through.
- Mobile map could be panned until a dark void showed above the neighbourhood.

## What was implausible
- Player got "Senior manager" the instant they arrived (no interview, no experience, no rivals).
- Nobody ever spoke to the player: 0 requests in 90 days. Cause found: arrivals/newcomers were never added to the weekly neighbour round, so no newcomer (NPC or player) grew local ties from the street.
- God "Heatwave" only nudged a mortality multiplier: no illness, no sleepless nights, no packed park. "Rent shock" changed rents silently (no letters, rows, moves).
- Goal pressures listened for event names that never fire (`home.rent_rise`, `work.fired`), so rent rises / job loss never fed people's goals.

## What was delightful
- A neighbour knocking on the newcomer's door ("popped round to ask if you need anything") with Yes / Maybe / Not now; the street map lighting up at dusk; the 1-hour-per-second scene.
- Inspector portraits, "Neighbours rally round X", news voice (when varied).

## Top 10 fixes, ranked by experience impact (status)
1. NPCs involve the player: newcomers join the neighbour round; neighbours greet them (agreeable/outgoing more often), pop round, later ask small favours; requests surface as inbox cards. DONE (4 requests/90 days, first inside ~6 weeks).
2. Storylines worth following: patterns rewritten on real event names; one story per protagonist, recency x variety ranked; headline "Priya & Tom: got together, moved in together, expecting a baby"; "Who to follow"; one-line story in the inspector. DONE.
3. Felt consequences, cause-linked: heatwave -> heat illness (old, ill, alone, top-floor), broken sleep, tempers at home, packed parks; rent shock -> a letter per household, couples row about money, goals (find flat/flatmate) via pressure; feed tags "because of: Heatwave". DONE.
4. Player hiring through NPC mechanisms (qualification, experience gate for management, competition from jobseekers, 5-21 day interview lag, rejection reasons). DONE.
5. Month-start clumping: labour reviews now spread over the month. DONE for labour; housing moves/notices, money cost-pressure and mental-health episodes still land on the 1st (feed folds them into one summary line). OPEN: spread them in sim/systems (owned by realism agent).
6. Text variety: ~120 new headline/body alternatives, shared quip pools, plain-English places/reasons/employers/jobs. DONE (more pools still welcome).
7. Human durations ("8 months here"), short segment names, short job labels, activity labels. DONE.
8. Map can no longer be panned into the void (clamped to edge). DONE.
9. OPEN: colleagues inviting a new hire (off-map workplaces have no on-map colleagues to simulate); gossip about the player appears only in Me > reputation, not in the feed.
10. OPEN: forum replies still tone-pooled, not topic-matched; loneliness/ties for 65+ still too high (realism agent).
