**Surcharges on corporate cards:** I couldn't open the guidance page, so this part is from the search snippet and my own background knowledge. The UK ban on card surcharges covers consumer credit and debit cards. As I understand it, it does not cover corporate or commercial cards, so they may be allowed to charge extra for yours. Any surcharge is meant to be limited to what the payment costs them to process. Please check this against current rules before relying on it.

**Questions for the call:**
1. Can the £500 deposit be paid by corporate card, for example through a payment link, rather than by bank transfer?
2. Is there a surcharge for corporate cards, and if so, how much (percentage or flat fee)?
3. Is the deposit refundable, and does that depend on the payment method?
