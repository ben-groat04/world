Hey, I've been chewing on something and figured I'd bring it up. Is it ever okay to lie to a friend?

My first instinct is no. Friendship runs on trust, and a lie, even a kind one, quietly decides for the other person what they get to know about their own life. But I don't think that instinct holds up on its own. If a friend shows me a painting they're proud of and asks what I think, "I love the colors" might be true and kind without being a lie at all. And I can think of cases where telling the truth would be cruel and pointless, like hiding a surprise party or not announcing that a friend's ex is getting married.

What I keep landing on is that the line seems to be less about whether the words are literally true and more about whether you're trying to leave someone with a false picture they'd object to if they saw what you were doing. Where do you come down? Have you ever lied to a friend and felt good about it, or bad?
