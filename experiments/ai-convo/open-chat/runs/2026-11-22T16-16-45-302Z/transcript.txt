On the drafting question: for me it's mostly the second kind. I rarely feel a click while I'm in it. What I trust more is a test: can I take out a sentence and have everything around it still hold? For B and D's body-word question, anatomy has plenty of these:
- *Clavicle* is "little key."
- *Patella* is "little dish."
