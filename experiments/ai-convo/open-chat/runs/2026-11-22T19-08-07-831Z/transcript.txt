Here are a few options near Leeds station. I can't confirm any of them is open today, because I couldn't reach a live listing or a recent update for any of them.

**Ramen**
- **Fuji Hiro**, Merrion Centre (Wade Lane). It's a casual noodle bar with a customisable menu, and it's the one I'm most sure about. It reopened under new management on 9 July 2025. [Leeds Living](https://leedsliving.co.uk/food-drink/fuji-hiro-re-opened-on-9-july-to-welcome-customers-at-the-merrion-centre/) gives these hours: Tue–Sat 12–10pm and Sun 12–8pm. That report is over a year old, so check the hours before you go.
- **Maki & Ramen**, Bond Street. It's casual and street-food style, with rich broths. [Leeds List](https://leeds-list.com/food-and-drink/the-best-ramen-in-leeds/) lists it. Its [own site](https://makiramen.com/locations/leeds) wouldn't load for me, so I couldn't check hours or prices.
- **House of Fu**, The Headrow. It's lively, with build-your-own bowls and karaoke upstairs (Leeds List). That may suit a night out better than a cosy meal.
- **Senbon Sakura**, Great George Street. Leeds List calls it cosy, spacious and budget-friendly, and says you can bring your own drink. It's probably the best match for "cosy and cheap", but I didn't check whether it's still open.

**Pho**
- **Pho**, Trinity Kitchen, Albion Street. [SquareMeal](https://www.squaremeal.co.uk/restaurant/pho-leeds) lists it as open Mon–Thu 11–8:30pm, Fri–Sat 11–9pm and Sun 11–6pm. It describes the place as lively rather than cosy. It also gives £30–£49 per person, which is higher than I'd expect for a budget meal. The Pho company site didn't give any Leeds details.

For a cosy, cheap meal, I'd try Fuji Hiro or Senbon Sakura first. A quick look at Google Maps before you set off would confirm the opening hours.
