The Llanberis path is about 9 miles return, or 4.5 miles each way. Allow roughly 2.5 to 4 hours up, and 5 to 7 hours for the round trip. The park authority lists a shorter distance, 6.8 miles, so the sources disagree a bit. It's the longest and gentlest route, and it's also the busiest, so the Ranger path will be quieter.

You don't need a car. There's no train to Llanberis itself, and the nearest mainline station is Bangor, about 10 miles away. From London there are direct trains to Bangor, around 3h 20m. I haven't checked live timetables. Sherpa times change by season, so look at the Eryri site before booking anything for March or April.
