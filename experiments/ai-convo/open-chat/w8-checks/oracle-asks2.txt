Q: What is the best way to get from Heathrow to central London after midnight?
  searches 1, fetches 0, fetches returning a page 0; fetched: -
  answer URL: https://www.heathrow.com/transport-and-directions/getting-to-central-london (not fetched)
  A: Night buses and taxis are your options after midnight. The N9 night bus runs about every 30 minutes from Heathrow to Trafalgar Square and takes roughly 60 to 80 minutes. Taxis or minicabs are faster but cost more. The Piccadilly line's Night Tube only runs on Friday and Saturday nights, and it doesn't serve Terminal 4.  https://www.heathrow.com/transport-and-directions/getting-to-central-london
